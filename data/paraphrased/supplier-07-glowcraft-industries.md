# Manufacturing Partner Overview — GlowCraft Industries

Thirteen years in operation from our site in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

## What we can make

We are a dry-format house. Our presses turn out compacts and pressed powders,
solid sticks, and cream-to-powder hybrids. We do not currently operate any
fluid-filling capability, and serums are outside what this site can produce; a
fluid line appears on our capital plan for 2027 but is not yet commissioned.

## Quality and compliance

Cosmetics GMP is certified to ISO 22716:2007 under certificate
IN-COS-22716-0655, issued 10 November 2023 and valid through 9 November 2026.

Of twenty-four audited runs, twenty passed and four were rejected — about
seventeen per cent rejected. Mean defect level on passing runs was 1.05 per cent.

## Commercial terms

Minimum first run is three thousand pieces. Manufacturing takes eleven calendar
days on average once artwork is approved.

Indicative cost on comparable articles is USD 38.90 each.

## Ethics

Our animal-testing-free declaration is on file, reference CF-2023-0088, dated
14 August 2023.
