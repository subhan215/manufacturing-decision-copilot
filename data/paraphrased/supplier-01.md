# Manufacturing Partner Overview — Supplier 1

Our Mumbai plant (Maharashtra state, India) has operated for fourteen years and
runs several parallel production halls.

## What we can make

Hall A is given over entirely to fluid preparations — we run dropper filling and
pump filling on the same line, and we have completed two prior serum programmes
for regional skincare brands under confidentiality. Hall B handles emulsions and
lotions; Hall C presses powders.

## Quality and compliance

We work to Cosmetics GMP under ISO 22716:2007. Our certificate, numbered
IN-COS-22716-0341, was issued on 12 March 2024 and remains in force through
11 March 2027. We additionally maintain ISO 9001:2015 (expires 30 November 2026).

Across the twenty-seven most recent audited runs, thirteen cleared inspection
outright, eight remain under re-inspection, and six were rejected — a rejection
proportion of roughly twenty-two percent. Defects on cleared batches averaged
1.80 per cent.

## Commercial terms

We will open an account at three thousand units for a first production run.
From sign-off on formulation and artwork, expect somewhere between twelve and
fourteen calendar days to manufacture; our measured mean across recent runs is
12.6 days.

Indicative manufacturing cost on comparable articles works out around USD 45.30
per unit.

## Ethics

A signed statement confirming we do not test on animals is held on file under
reference CF-2025-0091, dated 18 January 2025. We are not currently accredited by
any external cruelty-free scheme.
