# Manufacturing Partner Overview — Sunrise Personal Care Pvt Ltd

Twelve years supplying national brands from Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

## What we can make

Our lines are built for fluid preparations at volume, serums included, geared to
large-batch national-brand contracts.

## Quality and compliance

Cosmetics GMP certification to ISO 22716:2007 is held under certificate
IN-COS-22716-0499. It was issued on 18 April 2023 and its validity ran to
17 April 2026.

Thirty runs were audited: twenty-four passed, six were rejected, which is twenty
per cent rejected. Defects on passing runs averaged 1.40 per cent.

## Commercial terms

Our commercial policy sets a floor of twenty thousand pieces per article; the
plant is not set up to take pilot or launch-sized orders below that figure.
Production runs thirteen calendar days on average.

Indicative cost on comparable articles is USD 28.75 each.

## Ethics

Animal-testing-free declaration on file, reference CF-2024-0355.
